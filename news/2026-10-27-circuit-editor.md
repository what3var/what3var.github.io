---
title: A circuit editor in spark
tag: spark
---
spark can now draw and simulate circuits. A toolbox, an Edit and a Simulation tool strip, and probes that measure max, min, peak-to-peak, average, RMS and frequency right in the drawing.

The features that will probably stand out the most:

- **Circuits become objects in your code.** Simulate analog circuits and talk to them like any other class: inputs and outputs bound to pins are members you can read and drive.
- **Real time, or even faster.** The simulation keeps pace with the clock, and can run at several times real time — or in slow motion when you want to watch.
- **Vacuum tubes:** Koren triode and pentode and a vacuum diode, with real models.
- **Falstad import:** circuits from Falstad's simulator can be imported, including its newer XML format.

Two samples get you started: an RC filter and an audio fuzz.
